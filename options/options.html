Model and spending

Google plan

Picking a plan fills in the limits below. The free tier allows few requests (for the Flash-Lite models about 15 a minute and 500 a day): images still get a request each while there’s room, and only a backlog shares requests. If Google reports a lower limit, the extension keeps to it. Your project’s exact limits are in AI Studio.

Reading

Lettering

Text on a plain background, like a speech bubble, is painted over in the bubble’s colour and relettered. Text on artwork is erased and the translation is set in its place, in the original lettering’s colours with a solid outline.

Sites

Turn sites on from the toolbar button. They’re translated automatically on every visit and reload.
